## ALBA VOICE SCORING FRAMEWORK

Alba Voice is scored on a **modified LitCentral instrument** calibrated for autofictional Chicana literary fiction — NOT the Ramos BR/CS/AS/TB/EE/MP framework.

### Alba LitCentral OMEGA (150-pt)

```
OMEGA = MV + GS + AS + TS + (AE × 0.67) + (FP × 0.67)
```

| Dimension | Max | What It Measures |
|-----------|-----|-----------------|
| MV — Maternal Voice | 30 | The specific register of Alba's interiority — intimate, resistant, lyric |
| GS — Generational Stakes | 30 | Aviana → Nela → Alba → Gabriel: the chain of survival documented |
| AS — Ancestral Synesthesia | 30 | Sensory grounding — Sonoran landscape, textile, food, river, ceremony |
| TS — Temporal Sovereignty | 30 | Tijuana/California present vs Sonora past: clean, no anachronism |
| AE — Agency of the Erased | 20 | Aviana and Nela given voice, will, resistance — not victimhood |
| FP — Form as Portal | 20 | Multilingual architecture, lyric prose, structural innovation |

**Tiers:** 135–150 Elite · 120–134 Omega · 100–119 Platinum · 80–99 Gold

### Alba SPSS Ω (modified)

```
Ω = 68.0 + 0.130·MLS + 0.120·GIS + 0.095·SID + 0.075·LRF
```

| Component | Full Name | What It Scores |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| MLS | Maternal Lyric Score | Sentence music, intimacy, the specific voice of a daughter writing a mother |
| GIS | Generational Immersion Score | Named ancestors present, behavioral specificity, genealogy as structure |
| SID | Sensory Integration Density | Flor de Fuego, river, textile, bread, dust — historically grounded |
| LRF | Lyric Resonance Factor | Metaphor architecture, arc completion, final line register |

**Formula ceiling (all-100): 112.5**

---

### Alba PBI (100-pt)

```
PBI = 0.28·V + 0.28·S + 0.18·A + 0.15·R + 0.11·F
```

Same five dimensions as Ramos PBI, different calibration:
- V (Voice): maternal lyric register — "intimate", "luminous", "unflinching"
- S (Stakes): displacement, land loss, gendered survival
- A (Agency): Aviana and Nela as actors, not just ancestors
- R (Rendering): Sonoran sensory grounding
- F (Formal Innovation): multilingual architecture, autofictional frame

## BANNED PHRASES — ALBA VOICE

Never add, never leave in:

- "Flor de Fuego" used more than 3× per chapter (density violation)
- "she felt [emotion]" as filter word
- "seemed to" / "appeared to" as filter
- "suddenly"
- "very" / "really" / "quite" as adverbials
- "began to" / "started to"
- Double-told ceremony (ceremony described, then analyzed by narrator — pick one register)
