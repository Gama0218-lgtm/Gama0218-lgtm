# Perplexity Prompt — Yaqui Ceremony & Cosmology Research

## USE CASE
Researching authentic Yaqui Nation ceremonial practice, cosmology, and cultural context
for historical fiction set during the Vietnam War era (1965–1975).

---

## PROMPT TEMPLATE

```
I am researching Yaqui (Yoeme) Nation ceremonial traditions and cosmology for a historical fiction novel set during the Vietnam War. The protagonist is a Yaqui soldier navigating between two worlds. I need academically grounded, culturally respectful information.

Please search for and summarize:

1. **Huya ania and yoania** — the "wilderness world" and "flower world" in Yaqui cosmology. What do these realms mean? How do initiates enter them? What are the sensory markers (sound, smell, physical sensation) associated with crossing between them?

2. **Pahko ceremony structure** — the Deer Dance and its relationship to the flower world. What happens physically and spiritually during the ceremony? Who officiates? What role does the Maso (Deer Dancer) play?

3. **Surem** — the "little people" or ancient Yaqui ancestors. What is their relationship to the Yaqui people today? Do they appear in ceremony?

4. **Vietnam-era Yaqui veterans** — are there documented accounts of Yaqui men who served in Vietnam? Any oral histories or academic work on how Indigenous veterans reconciled military service with ceremonial obligation?

5. **Academic sources** — prioritize University of Arizona, NAU, UTEP, and Yaqui Nation tribal college publications. Note any sources that are restricted from non-tribal publication.

Format: numbered findings with source URLs. Flag any content that Yaqui cultural governance considers restricted from outsider publication.
```

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT TO REQUEST
Ask Perplexity to return:
- Numbered findings
- Source URL for each finding
- Explicit flag if content is culturally restricted

## GOVERNANCE NOTE
Before using any ceremony-specific content in the manuscript, Yaqui Nation consultation
is required (P0 gate, non-negotiable). Use research only to verify what is publicly
documented — do not extrapolate ceremony content beyond cited sources.
