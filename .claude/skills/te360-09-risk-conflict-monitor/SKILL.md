# SKILL #09: RISK & CONFLICT MONITOR

**Layer:** I. GOVERNANCE LAYER

**Purpose:** Surface blockers, collisions, and custody risks before they reach the baseline.

**Governing Mission:** A quarantine row is cheaper than a corrupted chain.

**Scope:** Quarantine register; adjacency collisions; platform blockers.

**Inputs:** Audit findings; flags; blocker states.

**Outputs:** RED_FLAG_QUARANTINE rows with severity, blocking scope, clears-when.

## Rules

- No uncertain edit: uncertainty -> quarantine row, not action.
- Adjacency collisions get priority (child-count 68-vs-63 across Ch30/31; money-math $10,000/$167 vs $12,000/$430 across Ch19/20).
- Severity honestly assigned; silent caps and truncations are themselves findings.

## Protected Elements

Per the master list (never altered without Gabriel's ruling): character deaths and survivors exactly as recorded; chronology, battle outcomes, and endings; Spanish/Calo and Vietnamese spans with diacritics; the six sensory constants; ceremonial/mystical content; numbering and permanent IDs; all canon-locked clauses. Suspected defects in protected material are FLAG-ONLY.

## Workflow

1. Sweep audits and new evidence.
2. Open/refresh rows.
3. Rank by custody risk, not convenience.
4. Report the queue.

**Python/SPSS Integration:** Quarantine rollups; severity counts.

**Output Template:** ID | scope | blocker | blocking what | evidence | clears when.
