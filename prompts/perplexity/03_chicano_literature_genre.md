# Perplexity Prompt — Chicano Literature & Magical Realism Genre Research

## USE CASE
Researching the Chicano literary tradition and magical realism conventions
to ensure the manuscript's narrative register aligns with its genre ancestors.

---

## PROMPT TEMPLATE

```
I am writing a Chicano Vietnam War historical fiction novel and need to understand
the literary tradition I am working within. Please research:

1. **Foundational Chicano novels featuring Vietnam or military service** — are there
   published Chicano novels that deal with Vietnam combat experience? Who are the authors?
   How do they handle the intersection of barrio identity, Catholicism, and war trauma?

2. **Rudolfo Anaya's technique in Bless Me, Ultima** — how does Anaya blend curandera
   mysticism with realist narrative without breaking the narrative voice? What specific
   techniques keep the supernatural grounded?

3. **Tomás Rivera's "...y no se lo tragó la tierra"** — how does Rivera handle
   fragmented memory and collective Chicano identity? What is the relationship between
   individual chapters and the whole?

4. **Magical realism in Chicano vs. Latin American tradition** — how does the Chicano
   magical realism tradition differ from García Márquez or Isabel Allende? Specifically:
   how do Chicano authors handle Indigenous spiritual elements vs. Catholic folk elements?

5. **Battle mysticism in literary fiction** — are there examples of literary fiction
   (any tradition) where combat is rendered as spiritual transformation without losing
   realist grounding? Specifically: how do authors avoid generic "warrior mysticism"
   shorthand while still conveying transcendence?

6. **Academic Chicano literary criticism** — what journals or critics are the primary
   voices for Chicano literary studies? (I need to verify my novel's register against
   scholarly frameworks, not just popular reception.)

Format: numbered findings, source URL, 2-3 sentence summary per finding.
```

---

## REGISTER NOTES FOR THIS MANUSCRIPT
The Ch.01 narrative uses an **analytical register** — sensor metaphor, "element style"
language, social physics framing. Any magical realism elements must be grounded through
this analytical frame. Generic mystical shorthand ("language older than words,"
"cosmic rhythm") breaks the chapter's scoring and its genre positioning.
