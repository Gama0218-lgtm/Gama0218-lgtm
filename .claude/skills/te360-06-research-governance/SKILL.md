# SKILL #06: RESEARCH GOVERNANCE

**Layer:** I. GOVERNANCE LAYER

**Purpose:** Oversee the research program: order of operations, source discipline, the fiction/history bright line.

**Governing Mission:** LOC before web. Register before re-verification. Bright line always.

**Scope:** All research tracks; USC packet work.

**Inputs:** Research questions, register state, standing rules.

**Outputs:** Governed research plans; research-hold rows; bright-line annotations.

## Rules

- Settled facts (Section 1) are not re-verified without new evidence.
- The Ghost Protocol and all invented apparatus stay labeled invention in supporting materials.
- Rule 8: correct factual overstatements immediately and specifically.
- The story may deviate where the data leads - canon evolution expected, ruled by Gabriel, tracked by version.

## Protected Elements

Per the master list (never altered without Gabriel's ruling): character deaths and survivors exactly as recorded; chronology, battle outcomes, and endings; Spanish/Calo and Vietnamese spans with diacritics; the six sensory constants; ceremonial/mystical content; numbering and permanent IDs; all canon-locked clauses. Suspected defects in protected material are FLAG-ONLY.

## Workflow

1. Frame the question.
2. Check register/settled facts.
3. LOC protocol, then wider search.
4. Adjudicate via 02.
5. Encode via 04; log holds.

**Python/SPSS Integration:** Search logs; register cross-checks.

**Output Template:** Question | register state | sources | status | hold?

## Governance

Gabriel T. Arce makes all canonical and editorial decisions; Claude/TE360 is verifier and builder. The Master Workbook + custody log are the source-of-truth register (custody log governs where the workbook is stale, pending sync). No model self-authorizes canon. RESTORE, NEVER REWRITE. Band 5,600-5,800; never pad. Every operation is preflight-gated, logged, hashed, and acceptance-gated.
