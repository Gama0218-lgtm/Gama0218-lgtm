# Perplexity Prompt — Sensory & Environmental Detail Verification

## USE CASE
Verifying specific sensory details for authenticity — smells, sounds, physical
sensations tied to Vietnam combat environments and Southwest US settings.

---

## PROMPT TEMPLATE

```
I am writing a Vietnam War novel and need to verify specific sensory details
for authenticity. Please research each of the following:

1. **Jasmine in Vietnam** — does jasmine grow in the Mekong Delta and Central
   Highlands regions? What does it smell like in proximity to burning? Are there
   veteran accounts that specifically mention jasmine smell during combat operations?
   What is the scientific name of the dominant jasmine species in South Vietnam?

2. **Napalm smell** — what do veteran accounts say about the specific smell of
   napalm (MK-77 incendiary)? Is "jasmine and napalm" a documented sensory
   combination in Vietnam War veteran memoirs or oral histories?

3. **Cordite smell** — is "cordite" the accurate term for the smell of discharged
   firearms in 1966–1968 Vietnam? (Cordite was largely replaced by other propellants
   by WWII — what is the technically accurate term for the propellant smell in
   M16/M60 ammunition of this era?)

4. **Copal incense** — is copal used in Yaqui ceremony specifically, or is it
   primarily Aztec/Mexica? What does copal smell like vs. other incenses?
   Are there documented accounts of Yaqui ceremonial use of copal?

5. **Sonoran Desert terrain specifics** — what are the dominant sensory features
   of the Sonoran Desert in southern Arizona/northern Sonora in summer?
   Specific plants, smells, sounds at dusk. What does the ground feel like underfoot?

6. **60Hz electrical hum** — is 60Hz the correct frequency for US institutional
   electrical hum (fluorescent lighting, transformers)? Would a 1966 US military
   base in Vietnam use US-standard 60Hz or local Vietnamese 50Hz electrical systems?

Format: numbered findings, source URL, verdict (confirmed accurate / needs adjustment / uncertain).
```
