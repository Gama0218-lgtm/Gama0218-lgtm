Prompt 01
Yaqui Ceremony & Cosmology
Academic
Huya ania · yoania · Pahko ceremony · Surem · Vietnam-era Yaqui veterans. Flag restricted content.
I am researching Yaqui (Yoeme) Nation ceremonial traditions and cosmology for a historical fiction novel set during the Vietnam War. The protagonist is a Yaqui soldier navigating between two worlds. I need academically grounded, culturally respectful information. Please search for and summarize: 1. Huya ania and yoania — the "wilderness world" and "flower world" in Yaqui cosmology. What do these realms mean? How do initiates enter them? What are the sensory markers (sound, smell, physical sensation) associated with crossing between them? 2. Pahko ceremony structure — the Deer Dance and its relationship to the flower world. What happens physically and spiritually during the ceremony? Who officiates? What role does the Maso (Deer Dancer) play? 3. Surem — the "little people" or ancient Yaqui ancestors. What is their relationship to the Yaqui people today? Do they appear in ceremony? 4. Vietnam-era Yaqui veterans — are there documented accounts of Yaqui men who served in Vietnam? Any oral histories or academic work on how Indigenous veterans reconciled military service with ceremonial obligation? 5. Academic sources — prioritize University of Arizona, NAU, UTEP, and Yaqui Nation tribal college publications. Note any sources that are restricted from non-tribal publication. Format: numbered findings with source URLs. Flag any content that Yaqui cultural governance considers restricted from outsider publication.

Prompt 02
Chicano Vietnam Historical
Academic
Casualty rates · weapons (M60 not M249) · ID cards · Khe Sanh · Chicano Moratorium · code-switching.
I am fact-checking a historical fiction novel about a Mexican-American (Chicano) soldier in Vietnam, 1965–1972. I need verified historical details to avoid anachronisms and ensure authentic representation. Please research and confirm: 1. Chicano casualty rates in Vietnam — what percentage of Vietnam casualties were Mexican-American? Was there documented disproportionate front-line deployment? Cite specific studies (Chicano Moratorium, NARA data, or academic sources). 2. Standard infantry weapons 1965–1968 — what rifle/machine gun combinations were standard for Army infantry units in the Central Highlands during this period? Specifically: was the M60 the standard squad automatic weapon in 1966–1967? (I need to verify against M249 SAW, which was not fielded until 1984.) 3. Military ID cards 1960s — what did standard Army ID cards (DD Form 2) look like in 1966–1967? Were they magnetic stripe, punched card, or plain laminate? (Magnetic stripe was not standard military issue until the 1980s.) 4. Khe Sanh timeline — the Siege of Khe Sanh ran January 21 – July 9, 1968. What units were involved? Was the 101st Airborne present, or primarily Marines? What was the terrain and weather during the siege? 5. Chicano Moratorium 1970 — what was the stated position of the Chicano Moratorium movement on Vietnam? Who were the key figures? What happened at the August 29, 1970 Los Angeles demonstration? 6. Spanglish / code-switching in 1960s–70s — what does linguistic research say about how Chicano soldiers code-switched between Spanish and English during this era? Were there documented patterns of borrowing vs. full switching? Format: numbered findings, source URL per finding, confidence level (confirmed / probable / uncertain).

Prompt 03
Chicano Literature & Genre
Writing
Anaya technique · Rivera fragmented memory · Chicano vs. Latin American magical realism · battle mysticism without register collapse.
I am writing a Chicano Vietnam War historical fiction novel and need to understand the literary tradition I am working within. Please research: 1. Foundational Chicano novels featuring Vietnam or military service — are there published Chicano novels that deal with Vietnam combat experience? Who are the authors? How do they handle the intersection of barrio identity, Catholicism, and war trauma? 2. Rudolfo Anaya's technique in Bless Me, Ultima — how does Anaya blend curandera mysticism with realist narrative without breaking the narrative voice? What specific techniques keep the supernatural grounded? 3. Tomás Rivera's "...y no se lo tragó la tierra" — how does Rivera handle fragmented memory and collective Chicano identity? What is the relationship between individual chapters and the whole? 4. Magical realism in Chicano vs. Latin American tradition — how does the Chicano magical realism tradition differ from García Márquez or Isabel Allende? Specifically: how do Chicano authors handle Indigenous spiritual elements vs. Catholic folk elements? 5. Battle mysticism in literary fiction — are there examples of literary fiction (any tradition) where combat is rendered as spiritual transformation without losing realist grounding? Specifically: how do authors avoid generic "warrior mysticism" shorthand while still conveying transcendence? 6. Academic Chicano literary criticism — what journals or critics are the primary voices for Chicano literary studies? (I need to verify my novel's register against scholarly frameworks, not just popular reception.) Format: numbered findings, source URL, 2-3 sentence summary per finding.

Prompt 05
Sensory Detail Verification
Academic
Jasmine + napalm · cordite accuracy · copal in Yaqui ceremony · Sonoran Desert dusk · 60Hz institutional hum.
I am writing a Vietnam War novel and need to verify specific sensory details for authenticity. Please research each of the following: 1. Jasmine in Vietnam — does jasmine grow in the Mekong Delta and Central Highlands regions? What does it smell like in proximity to burning? Are there veteran accounts that specifically mention jasmine smell during combat operations? What is the scientific name of the dominant jasmine species in South Vietnam? 2. Napalm smell — what do veteran accounts say about the specific smell of napalm (MK-77 incendiary)? Is "jasmine and napalm" a documented sensory combination in Vietnam War veteran memoirs or oral histories? 3. Cordite smell — is "cordite" the accurate term for the smell of discharged firearms in 1966–1968 Vietnam? (Cordite was largely replaced by other propellants by WWII — what is the technically accurate term for the propellant smell in M16/M60 ammunition of this era?) 4. Copal incense — is copal used in Yaqui ceremony specifically, or is it primarily Aztec/Mexica? What does copal smell like vs. other incenses? Are there documented accounts of Yaqui ceremonial use of copal? 5. Sonoran Desert terrain specifics — what are the dominant sensory features of the Sonoran Desert in southern Arizona/northern Sonora in summer? Specific plants, smells, sounds at dusk. What does the ground feel like underfoot? 6. 60Hz electrical hum — is 60Hz the correct frequency for US institutional electrical hum (fluorescent lighting, transformers)? Would a 1966 US military base in Vietnam use US-standard 60Hz or local Vietnamese 50Hz electrical systems? Format: numbered findings, source URL, verdict (confirmed accurate / needs adjustment / uncertain).
