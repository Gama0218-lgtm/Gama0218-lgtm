## III. DUP-001 CONFIRMATION — CRITICAL / HIGHEST PRIORITY

**What the jury sees:** Two adjacent chapters that feel like the same scene twice. Regardless of PBI score, a jury reader moving from Ch17 to Ch18 will register the déjà vu — and it will suppress subjective confidence in the manuscript's structural control.
