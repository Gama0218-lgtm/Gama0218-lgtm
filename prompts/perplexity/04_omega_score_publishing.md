# Perplexity Prompt — Literary Scoring, Publishing & Manuscript Readiness

## USE CASE
Researching the publishing landscape for Chicano Vietnam War literary fiction —
market positioning, comparable titles, and agent/publisher targeting.

---

## PROMPT TEMPLATE

```
I have completed a 45-chapter literary fiction manuscript: a Vietnam War novel
centered on a Chicano/Yaqui protagonist. I need to understand the current
publishing landscape for this type of book.

Please research:

1. **Recent Chicano/Latino literary fiction — Vietnam or military — published 2015–2025**
   — what titles have been published? Which publishers acquired them? What were
   the advance ranges for debut literary fiction in this space?

2. **Literary agents actively seeking Chicano or Latino literary fiction** — which
   agents at major agencies have recent sales in this space? (Look for Publishers
   Marketplace deal announcements, MSWL posts, or agency website wish lists.)

3. **Independent presses specializing in Chicano literature** — Arte Público Press,
   Bilingual Press, Cinco Puntos Press — what are their current submission windows
   and acquisition criteria? Are they open to 45-chapter full-length literary fiction?

4. **Vietnam War literary fiction market 2020–2025** — has this sub-genre grown or
   contracted? What comparable titles (non-Chicano) have been breakout successes?
   What are agents and editors saying about Vietnam War fiction today?

5. **Sensitivity readers for Indigenous (Yaqui) content** — are there services or
   individual readers who specialize in Yaqui or Southwest Indigenous review for
   literary fiction? (This manuscript requires Yaqui Nation consultation before
   submission — I need parallel professional sensitivity review.)

6. **MFA programs with Chicano literature focus** — which MFA programs have faculty
   who specifically work in Chicano literary tradition? (For potential blurb contacts
   or workshop submission.)

Format: numbered findings, source URL, actionable next step per finding.
```

---

## MANUSCRIPT STATS (for context when evaluating fit)
- 45 chapters, literary fiction
- Protagonist: Chicano/Yaqui Vietnam veteran
- Register: analytical, sensor-grounded, social physics framing
- Comparable authors: Anaya, Rivera, Tim O'Brien (register), Viet Thanh Nguyen (market)
- Governance requirement: Yaqui Nation consultation before submission (non-negotiable)
