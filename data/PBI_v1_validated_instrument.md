## II. VALIDATION — WHY THIS FORMULA IS THE CORRECT APPROACH

**The Pulitzer's Own Statement (pulitzer.org/FAQ):**
> "There are no set criteria for the judging of the Prizes. The definitions of each category are the only guidelines. It is left up to the nominating juries and the Pulitzer Prize Board to determine exactly what makes a work 'distinguished.'"

**The Fiction Category Definition (pulitzer.org/administration):**
> "For distinguished fiction by an American author, preferably dealing with American life."

**The Pre-Judging Protocol (pulitzer.org/administration):**
> "Before they begin reviewing submissions, jurors must agree upon a set of criteria that will be used to assess the entries. These criteria could include difficulty, novelty and impact."

**What This Means:**
- No prescribed rubric exists. The jury invents criteria each cycle.
- The only valid external baseline is empirical — derived from what juries actually chose and how they described those choices in citation language.
- PBI is therefore the correct methodology — it reverse-engineers the implicit rubric from ten years of jury decisions.
- "Difficulty, novelty and impact" — the three named examples from the Pulitzer's own process description — map directly to PBI dimensions: Difficulty → Voice (V), Novelty → Formal Innovation (F), Impact → Stakes/Erasure (S) + Agency (A).

## III. THE FIVE DIMENSIONS — DEFINITIONS AND JURY ANCHORS

### V — VOICE (Weight: 0.28)

**Definition:** Distinctive prose register — sentence rhythm, element style, tonal control, active construction. The quality that makes a reader know who is speaking within three sentences.

**Jury language (all 11 winners):**
- "spare and devastating" (Whitehead, 2020)
- "majestic, polyphonic" (Erdrich, 2021)
- "mordant, linguistically deft" (Cohen, 2022)
- "wise, unwavering voice" (Kingsolver, 2023)
- "breathless" (Kraus, 2026)
- "elegant and fiercely honest" (Nguyen, 2016 — Caputo blurb)
- "accomplished" (Everett, 2025)
- "beautifully rendered" (Phillips, 2024)

**Why 0.28:** Present in 11/11 citations. Always the lead characterization or within the first clause. No winner has ever been cited without a voice descriptor. This is the jury's first filter.

**SGT Ramos mapping:** CLS component (Omega). Three-register code-switching architecture (ceremony Yaqui, narrative English, military jargon). 99% active voice. Zero banned phrases.

### S — STAKES / ERASURE (Weight: 0.28)

**Definition:** The historical, social, or institutional stakes the novel addresses. What is at risk in the world outside the novel. What silence the novel breaks.

**Jury language (11/11 winners):**
- "reform school... perseverance, dignity and redemption" (Whitehead)
- "halt the displacement and elimination of Native peoples" (Erdrich)
- "ambiguities of experience" (Cohen)
- "poverty, addiction, institutional failures" (Kingsolver)
- "family, wealth and ambition... capitalism is king" (Diaz)
- "the absurdity of racial supremacy... search for family and freedom" (Everett)
- "aftermath of the Civil War... struggle to heal" (Phillips)
- "World War I" (Kraus)
- "legacy of Vietnam" (Nguyen)

**Why 0.28:** Equal weight to Voice. Present in every citation. The jury always names the stakes — what real-world silence or injustice the novel confronts. No winner has ever been cited as "beautiful writing about nothing."

**SGT Ramos mapping:** The 349 anomaly. DCAS database erasure. Project 100,000. The mathematics of institutional disappearance. This dimension is the manuscript's strongest asset — no other novel on the shelf has a documented forensic spine.

### A — AGENCY OF THE ERASED (Weight: 0.18)

**Definition:** Whether the novel gives voice, interiority, and self-determination to the people the historical record erased. Not sympathy — agency.

**Jury language (9/11 winners):**
- "gives agency to Jim" (Everett — the most explicit statement)
- "dignity and redemption" (Whitehead)
- "halt the displacement and elimination" (Erdrich)
- "struggle to heal" (Phillips)
- "wise, unwavering voice relates his encounters" (Kingsolver — the boy narrates his own story)

**Why 0.18:** Distinct from Stakes. Stakes = what's wrong in the world. Agency = does the novel let the erased person tell it themselves, or does someone else narrate their suffering?

**SGT Ramos mapping:** 9 named dead in Ch.01, every one individualized. George narrates his own liberation. The dead are not mourned by others — they are present, named, and accounted for by someone who was there. The "No" at the headband — refusal of forgiveness, earning of liberation — is the agency beat.

### R — RENDERING / SENSORY SPECIFICITY (Weight: 0.15)

**Definition:** Sensory immersion — the physical, tactile, olfactory, and auditory world on the page. Historically accurate, culturally specific, non-generic sensory grounding.

**Jury language (8/11 winners):**
- "beautifully rendered" (Phillips — used twice in one citation)
- "spare and devastating" (Whitehead — "spare" = every sensory detail earns its place)
- "volatile as its tightly-wound plot" (Cohen — physicality)
- "laced with irony" (Nguyen context — the compound sensation of humor and atrocity simultaneously)

**Why 0.15:** Present in 8/11 but rarely leads the citation. Voice gets you in. Stakes keeps you in. Rendering wins the tiebreak.

**SGT Ramos mapping:** SII component (Omega). Current gap. 3/6 sensory constants confirmed (copal, copper, 60Hz). Three missing: jasmine-napalm, diesel-sweat, yerba buena. Three inserts close this.

### F — FORMAL INNOVATION (Weight: 0.11)

**Definition:** Structural or genre-level ambition — polyphony, linked narratives, genre-blending, non-linear architecture.

**Jury language (7/11 winners):**
- "stylistic tour-de-force that blends such genres as allegory, magical realism and science fiction into a cohesive whole, told in a single sentence" (Kraus — most explicit)
- "polyphonic... dexterity and imagination" (Erdrich)
- "linked narratives rendered in different literary styles" (Diaz)
- "linguistically deft... volatile as its tightly-wound plot" (Cohen)
- "reconsideration of Huckleberry Finn" (Everett — formal conceit of retelling)
- "masterful recasting of David Copperfield" (Kingsolver — formal conceit)

**Why 0.11:** Present in 7/11 but never alone sufficient. Innovation without voice or stakes doesn't win.

**SGT Ramos mapping:** Code-switching architecture (three registers). Yaqui cosmology as ontological frame, not metaphor. IBM System/360 terminal as structural antagonist. The forensic spine. This dimension is baked into the design — no revision needed.
