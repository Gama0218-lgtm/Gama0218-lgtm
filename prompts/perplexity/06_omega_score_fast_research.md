# Perplexity Prompt — FAST RESEARCH (Single Chapter, Score-Targeted)

## USE CASE
Quick targeted research before revising a specific chapter to lift its Omega score.
Use this when you have 30–60 minutes before a revision session.

---

## PROMPT TEMPLATE

```
I am revising [CHAPTER TITLE] of a Vietnam War literary fiction novel.
This chapter covers: [1-2 sentence summary of what the chapter does].

I need 3–5 verified facts or sensory details I can insert to strengthen
[choose: historical authenticity / sensory grounding / character specificity / cultural accuracy].

Specifically I need:

- [SPECIFIC DETAIL 1 you're unsure about]
- [SPECIFIC DETAIL 2]
- [SPECIFIC DETAIL 3]

For each, give me:
1. The verified fact
2. A single source URL
3. One example sentence showing how the detail could appear in literary prose
   (third-person limited, present-to-past reflection, analytical register —
   sensor metaphor, not generic mystical language)

Keep responses tight — one paragraph per finding, source, example sentence. No summaries.
```

---

## HOW TO USE
1. Fill in `[CHAPTER TITLE]` and `[1-2 sentence summary]`
2. Choose your target improvement (historical / sensory / character / cultural)
3. List the 3 specific things you're uncertain about
4. Paste into Perplexity with "Focus: Academic" mode on

## EXAMPLE FILLED VERSION

```
I am revising "Chapter 39: When Compadres Fall" of a Vietnam War literary fiction novel.
This chapter covers: two Chicano soldiers, one wounded, the other refusing to leave him,
in the Central Highlands, 1968.

I need 3–5 verified sensory details I can insert to strengthen historical authenticity
and sensory grounding.

Specifically I need:

- What does the Central Highlands terrain smell like in the rainy season (May–September)?
  Specifically: soil, vegetation, rain on hot metal.
- What did Army medevac radio procedure sound like in 1968? What words/codes were used?
- Is copal smoke plausible in this terrain — would a Chicano/Yaqui soldier carry it?
  Or is a different incense/herb more likely?

For each, give me:
1. The verified fact
2. A single source URL
3. One example sentence showing how the detail could appear in literary prose
   (third-person limited, present-to-past reflection, analytical register —
   sensor metaphor, not generic mystical language)
```

---

## SCORING CONTEXT
Ch.39 gap to Ω=111: **3.94 points**. SII (sensory immersion) is the only weak component.
One 200-word sensory anchor insert projects to Ω≥111.0 — this is the fastest path to
the first 111 chapter in the manuscript.
