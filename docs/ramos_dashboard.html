III · YAQUI COSMOLOGY — Academic Sources (Five Worlds, Deer Dance, Ceremony)
The sacred deer moves from the yo ania to the huya ania, and dances in the sea ania (flower world). When killed in the dance, it transforms into flowers — renewal, interconnectedness, sacrifice. The Surem are those who refused baptism and fled to preserve yoania — a relationship with the world itself.
